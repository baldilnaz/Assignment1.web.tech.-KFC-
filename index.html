About This Branch

This site is dedicated to the KFC branch inside the Nurly Zhol railway station in Astana. It serves famous fried chicken made with 11 herbs and spices. It is a very convenient spot for travelers waiting for their train, as well as students looking for a quick lunch.

Orders are prepared quickly at the counter so passengers do not miss their trains. The staff keeps the table area clean even during busy evening hours.
